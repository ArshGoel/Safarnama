Gujarat Heritage & Coastlines

7 Days Complete Circuit • Ahmedabad • Dwarka • Somnath • Sasan Gir • Rajkot

Day 1

Arrival in Ahmedabad & Cultural Landmarks

  • Arrive at Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel International Airport / Railway Station and check into your hotel.
  • Visit the serene Sabarmati Ashram on the riverfront, where Mahatma Gandhi led the independence struggle.
  • Explore Sidi Saiyyed Mosque, famed globally for the intricately carved "Tree of Life" stone jali.
  • Evening stroll and street food delights along the illuminated Kankaria Lake.
  • Overnight stay in Ahmedabad.
Day 2

Ahmedabad to the Sacred Kingdom of Dwarka

  • Scenic morning coastal highway drive to Dwarka across Saurashtra peninsula.
  • Check in to your hotel and prepare for Darshan at the magnificent 5-story Dwarkadhish Jagat Mandir.
  • Attend the divine evening Aarti and listen to temple bells ringing against the Arabian Sea.
  • Sunset walk along Gomti Ghat where the holy river meets the sea.
  • Overnight stay in Dwarka.
Day 3

Bet Dwarka Island & Nageshwar Jyotirlinga

  • Morning boat ride from Okha port to Bet Dwarka island across turquoise waters.
  • Seek blessings at Nageshwar Jyotirlinga, one of the 12 sacred Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva.
  • Visit Gopi Talav and the holy Rukmini Devi Temple.
  • Relax at Shivrajpur Blue Flag certified beach with crystal clear waters.
  • Overnight stay in Dwarka.
Day 4

Dwarka to Somnath via Porbandar

  • Drive towards Somnath along the coastal belt of Kathiawar.
  • Stop at Porbandar to visit Kirti Mandir (Mahatma Gandhi’s birthplace) and Sudama Temple.
  • Reach Somnath and witness the awe-inspiring seaside Somnath Jyotirlinga Temple.
  • Experience the mesmerizing evening Light and Sound Show narrating the temple’s historic resurrection.
  • Overnight stay in Somnath.
Day 5

Somnath to Gir National Park (Asiatic Lions)

  • Drive into the deciduous dry forests of Sasan Gir, the only natural habitat of the Asiatic Lion.
  • Embark on an exhilarating open-top Jeep Safari through the Gir Wildlife Sanctuary.
  • Spot wild lions, leopards, sambar deer, nilgai, and exotic migratory birds.
  • Evening traditional Kathiyawadi dinner with organic millet rotla and garlic chutney.
  • Overnight stay at a wildlife resort in Sasan Gir.
Day 6

Gir to Rajkot Royal Heritage

  • Drive to the vibrant cultural city of Rajkot.
  • Visit Kaba Gandhi No Delo, the ancestral house of Mahatma Gandhi showcasing rare photographs.
  • Explore Watson Museum and the majestic Jubilee Garden.
  • Shop for famous traditional Bandhani sarees, silver crafts, and Patola silks in Rajkot markets.
  • Overnight stay in Rajkot.
Day 7

Rajkot to Ahmedabad & Farewell

  • Drive back to Ahmedabad and stop at the 15th-century subterranean marvel: Adalaj Stepwell.
  • Marvel at the 5-story deep Solanki architecture and intricate stone carvings.
  • Pick up Gujarati sweets (Mohanthal, Khakhra) for loved ones.
  • Transfer to Ahmedabad airport or railway station for departure with cherished memories.
