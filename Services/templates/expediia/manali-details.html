Manali Alpine Valley Expedition

4 Days Thrills • Solang Valley • Rohtang Pass • Hadimba Temple • Jogini Waterfalls Trek

Day 1

Arrival, Hadimba Sanctuary & Old Manali

  • Arrive in Manali and check into your cozy riverside wooden cottage or resort.
  • Walk through the serene deodar cedar forests to the ancient wooden Hadimba Devi Temple.
  • Explore the peaceful grounds of the Himalayan Nyinmapa Tibetan Buddhist Monastery.
  • Spend the evening cafe-hopping in Old Manali enjoying live rock music and wood-fired pizzas.
  • Overnight stay in Manali.
Day 2

Solang Valley Adventures & High Mountain Pass

  • Early drive up to Solang Valley, India’s premier hub for adventure sports.
  • Tandem paragliding flight offering bird's-eye views of snow-draped Pir Panjal range.
  • Optional snow scooter rides and ropeway cable car ascent to Mount Phatru.
  • Travel through the iconic Atal Tunnel to Sissu for jaw-dropping glacier waterfalls.
  • Overnight stay in Manali.
Day 3

Jogini Waterfalls Nature Trek & Vashisht Hot Springs

  • Morning guided trek from Vashisht village through apple orchards and pine groves to Jogini Waterfall.
  • Stand beneath the cascading misty water of the multi-tier fall.
  • Return to Vashisht to experience the therapeutic natural sulfur hot water springs.
  • Stroll along the buzzing Mall Road for evening souvenir shopping.
  • Overnight stay in Manali.
Day 4

Beas River Rafting, Kullu Valley & Departure

  • Drive down towards Kullu and gear up with lifejackets and helmets for grade 3 white-water rafting on the Beas River.
  • Visit Naggar Castle, a 500-year-old stone and timber architectural treasure overlooking the Beas valley.
  • Check out the Nicholas Roerich Art Gallery in Naggar.
  • Transfer to Bhuntar airport or board luxury overnight sleeper coach for return.
