Himachal Mountain Panorama

5 Days Alpine Escape • Shimla Colonial Mall • Kufri Snow Viewpoint • Kullu Valley • Manali Pine Woods

Day 1

Arrival in Shimla & The Colonial Ridge

  • Drive up from Chandigarh or fly into Jubbarhatti Airport, arriving at the Queen of Hills: Shimla.
  • Stroll along the historic vehicle-free Mall Road and the Ridge.
  • Visit the neo-Gothic Christ Church and the historic Gaiety Theatre.
  • Take the Jakhoo Ropeway up to the Jakhoo Hanuman Temple for panoramic peak views.
  • Overnight stay in Shimla.
Day 2

Kufri Highland Snow Excursion & Chail

  • Morning trip to Kufri, situated at 2,622 meters above sea level amidst Himalayan cedar groves.
  • Enjoy horse riding up to Mahasu Peak and go tobogganing on seasonal snow slopes.
  • Visit the Himalayan Wildlife Zoo home to snow leopards, monal pheasants, and brown bears.
  • Afternoon drive to Chail to see the world's highest cricket ground and Chail Palace.
  • Overnight stay in Shimla.
Day 3

Scenic Trans-Himalayan Drive: Shimla to Manali

  • Depart Shimla along the National Highway traversing picturesque gorges and river valleys.
  • Stop at the Pandoh Dam reservoir and pass through the Aut tunnel.
  • Visit Kullu Shawl weaving factories and participate in exhilarating river rafting on the Beas River.
  • Arrive in Manali surrounded by towering snow-clad peaks.
  • Overnight stay in Manali.
Day 4

Solang Valley & Atal Tunnel Engineering Marvel

  • Explore Solang Valley for paragliding, zorbing, ATV quad biking, and skiing.
  • Drive through the 9.02 km long Atal Tunnel under Rohtang Pass into the otherworldly Lahaul Valley (Sissu).
  • Witness the spectacular frozen Sissu Waterfall and hanging glaciers.
  • Evening visit to the Tibetan Monastery and bustling Old Manali market.
  • Overnight stay in Manali.
Day 5

Hadimba Temple, Vashisht Springs & Departure

  • Visit the 16th-century wooden Pagoda-style Hadimba Devi Temple surrounded by towering deodar forests.
  • Dip your feet in the natural medicinal hot sulfur springs of Vashisht village.
  • Shop for fresh local apples, Himalayan honey, and handmade woolens.
  • Transfer to Bhuntar Airport (Kullu) or board luxury Volvo bus for departure.
