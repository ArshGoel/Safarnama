Goa Tropical Paradise

4 Days Sunny Getaway • North Goa Beaches • Fort Aguada • Panjim Latin Quarter • Heritage Cathedrals

Day 1

Arrival in Goa & North Beaches Relaxation

  • Arrive at Goa Airport (Dabolim/MOPA) or Madgaon railway station and check into your beach resort.
  • Head straight to Calangute and Baga beach to soak in the ocean breeze and golden sands.
  • Sip on fresh tender coconut water and explore beachside cafes with live acoustic music.
  • Witness a breathtaking sunset into the Arabian Sea followed by fresh Goan seafood dinner.
  • Overnight stay in North Goa.
Day 2

Portuguese Fortresses, Watersports & Vagator

  • Morning exploration of the 17th-century Fort Aguada and its historic hilltop lighthouse.
  • Water sports thrills at Anjuna or Baga beach: jet skiing, parasailing, and banana boat rides.
  • Climb up to Chapora Fort (the famous Dil Chahta Hai viewpoint) overlooking the Ozran coast.
  • Evening visit to the vibrant Anjuna Flea Market for handcrafted souvenirs, jewelry, and boho apparel.
  • Overnight stay in North Goa.
Day 3

Historic Old Goa Heritage & Fontainhas Latin Quarter

  • Visit the UNESCO World Heritage Basilica of Bom Jesus housing the sacred relics of St. Francis Xavier.
  • Admire the monumental Se Cathedral and Church of St. Francis of Assisi.
  • Stroll through Fontainhas, the colorful Portuguese Latin quarter in Panaji with cobblestone streets and pastel heritage villas.
  • Sunset Mandovi River Cruise featuring traditional Goan folk dances (Dekhni & Fugdi).
  • Overnight stay in Panaji / Central Goa.
Day 4

South Goa Tranquility & Departure

  • Morning visit to the tranquil crescent bay of Palolem Beach or butterfly beach.
  • Indulge in an authentic Goan fish thali or bebinca dessert at a local tavern.
  • Shop for roasted cashew nuts, feni, and Goan spices at the Mapusa or Panaji market.
  • Transfer to the airport or railway station for your onward journey.
