Chardham Yatra Pilgrimage

10 Days Himalayan Odyssey • Yamunotri • Gangotri • Kedarnath • Badrinath • Rishikesh

Day 1

Arrival in Holy Haridwar & Ganga Aarti

  • Arrive at Haridwar railway station or Dehradun Jolly Grant Airport.
  • Check into your hotel and take a holy dip in the sacred waters of the Ganges at Brahmakund.
  • Attend the divine evening Ganga Aarti at Har Ki Pauri with thousands of floating earthen lamps (diyas).
  • Briefing by yatra tour guides on high-altitude preparation and gear.
  • Overnight stay in Haridwar.
Day 2

Haridwar to Barkot (Foot of Yamunotri)

  • Depart early morning on the mountain ascent via Mussoorie and Kempty Falls.
  • Drive along the winding Yamuna valley towards Barkot.
  • Enjoy pristine views of the Bandarpoonch peak and lush Garhwal Himalayan foothills.
  • Rest and acclimatize at Barkot hill station.
  • Overnight stay in Barkot.
Day 3

Yamunotri Dham Darshan & Trek

  • Drive to Janki Chatti and commence the scenic 6 km trek (by foot, pony, or doli) to Yamunotri Dham.
  • Take a holy dip in the Surya Kund thermal hot spring and cook rice prasad tied in muslin cloth.
  • Pray at the black marble idol of Goddess Yamuna and Divya Shila rock.
  • Trek back down to Janki Chatti and return to Barkot.
  • Overnight stay in Barkot.
Day 4

Barkot to Uttarkashi (Kashi of the North)

  • Scenic drive across the Radi Top pass towards the Bhagirathi River basin.
  • Reach Uttarkashi, known as the abode of saints and yogis.
  • Visit the ancient Vishwanath Temple featuring a massive historic copper trident (Trishul).
  • Relax and meditate along the banks of the Bhagirathi river.
  • Overnight stay in Uttarkashi.
Day 5

Gangotri Dham Darshan & Bhagirathi Valley

  • Drive along the thrilling high-altitude gorge passing through Harsil Apple Valley.
  • Take a holy bath in the icy waters of the Bhagirathi river at Gangotri.
  • Attend pooja and Darshan at the 18th-century white granite Gangotri Temple dedicated to Goddess Ganga.
  • Observe the submerged Shivling underwater near Bhagirath Shila.
  • Return to Uttarkashi for overnight stay.
Day 6

Uttarkashi to Guptkashi

  • Traverse the scenic Garhwal terrain connecting Bhagirathi valley to the Mandakini valley.
  • Pass by the gigantic Tehri Dam reservoir with expansive emerald-green mountain waters.
  • Arrive at Guptkashi, the historic base for Kedarnath pilgrims.
  • Visit the ancient Ardhanarishwar temple and Kashi Vishwanath shrine.
  • Overnight stay in Guptkashi.
Day 7

Guptkashi to Kedarnath Jyotirlinga Darshan

  • Travel to Gaurikund and begin the unforgettable 16 km mountain pilgrimage trek to Kedarnath (or via helicopter).
  • Trek past gushing waterfalls, snowfields, and panoramic views of the Kedar Dome.
  • Reach Kedarnath Dham standing tall before gigantic snow-capped Himalayan peaks.
  • Attend the mystical evening Aarti inside the ancient stone temple of Lord Shiva.
  • Overnight stay in Kedarnath camp / guesthouse.
Day 8

Kedarnath to Badrinath Dham

  • Morning darshan at Kedarnath and descent back to Gaurikund.
  • Drive via Joshimath and Vishnuprayag to Badrinath, nestled between Nar and Narayana mountain ranges.
  • Bathe in the natural sulfur hot spring Tapt Kund.
  • Seek blessings of Lord Badri Narayan at the colorfully painted Badrinath Temple.
  • Visit Mana Village, the last Indian village before the Tibet border, and Bheem Pul over Saraswati River.
  • Overnight stay in Badrinath.
Day 9

Badrinath to Rishikesh (Yoga Capital of the World)

  • Drive down the Alaknanda valley passing Panch Prayag confluences (Karnaprayag, Rudraprayag, Devprayag).
  • Arrive in Rishikesh, the world capital of Yoga and meditation.
  • Walk across Ram Jhula and Lakshman Jhula suspension bridges.
  • Attend the soul-stirring Triveni Ghat Ganga Aarti.
  • Overnight stay in Rishikesh.
Day 10

Departure with Divine Blessings

  • Morning meditation session or river rafting excursion in Rishikesh.
  • Transfer to Dehradun Airport or Haridwar Railway station for your onward return journey.
