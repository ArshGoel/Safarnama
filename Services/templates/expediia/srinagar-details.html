Srinagar & Kashmir Valley

4 Days Paradise on Earth • Dal Lake Shikara • Mughal Terraces • Gulmarg Gondola • Pahalgam Meadows

Day 1

Arrival in Srinagar & Traditional Houseboat Check-in

  • Arrive at Sheikh ul-Alam International Airport in Srinagar and transfer to Dal Lake or Nigeen Lake.
  • Board your traditional hand-carved cedar wood Kashmiri Houseboat.
  • Embark on a serene sunset Shikara ride across the tranquil waters of Dal Lake.
  • Glide past the floating lotus gardens, floating vegetable markets, and Char Chinar island.
  • Savor an authentic Kashmiri Wazwan dinner with aromatic Kahwa saffron tea.
  • Overnight stay on the Houseboat.
Day 2

Royal Mughal Terraces & Shankaracharya Hill

  • Drive up to the ancient Shankaracharya Temple atop Gopadari Hill for an aerial view of Srinagar city.
  • Tour the famous terraced Mughal Gardens built by Emperor Jahangir and Shah Jahan: Nishat Bagh (Garden of Bliss) and Shalimar Bagh (Abode of Love).
  • Visit Chashme Shahi known for its freshwater royal spring.
  • Explore Pari Mahal (Palace of Fairies) overlooking the Dal Lake.
  • Overnight stay in Srinagar.
Day 3

Gulmarg Highland Alpine Meadow & Gondola Ride

  • Excursion to Gulmarg (Meadow of Flowers), located at 2,730 meters above sea level.
  • Ride the world's second-highest cable car: the Gulmarg Gondola.
  • Phase 1 ascends to Kongdoori Valley, and Phase 2 ascends to the thrilling 3,980m peak of Apharwat.
  • Experience pristine snow fields, snowmobiling, or winter skiing.
  • Walk through the golf course and visit the historic St. Mary's Church.
  • Overnight stay in Srinagar.
Day 4

Old City Heritage, Saffron Markets & Departure

  • Visit the Jamia Masjid in Downtown Srinagar with its 378 imposing deodar wood pillars.
  • Cross the historical wooden Zaina Kadal bridge over the Jhelum river.
  • Shop for pure Kashmiri saffron, dried walnuts, pashmina shawls, and papier-mâché crafts at Lal Chowk.
  • Transfer to Srinagar Airport for your return flight with unforgettable memories.
